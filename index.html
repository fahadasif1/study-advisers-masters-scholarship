FAQ

Before you book

Anything else? Call us on 0333 011 9288 and an adviser will help.

Is the event really free?

Yes. Entry, dinner and the scholarship draw are all free. There is nothing to pay at any point.

Can I bring a friend or family member?

Each ticket admits one student. If a friend wants to come, they should send their own seat request using this page.

When will I get my ticket?

Our team reviews every request and sends tickets by email or SMS before the event. Check your spam folder if you have not heard from us.

What ID do I need to bring?

Bring an original photo ID: your passport or a UK photo driving licence. Photocopies and photos on your phone are not accepted. The name must match the name on your seat request.

When do seat requests close?

At 23:59 the day before each event: Wednesday 28 October for London, Sunday 1 November for Birmingham and Wednesday 4 November for Manchester. Seats are limited, so requests may close earlier if a venue is full.

What does the scholarship cover?

The full tuition fees, with no upper limit, for one Master's (postgraduate taught) course at a Study Advisers partner university in the UK. It does not cover living costs, accommodation, visa fees, the Immigration Health Surcharge or travel. The winner must meet the university's normal admission requirements.

Do I have to be at the event to win?

Yes. Only guests who are checked in with original photo ID on the night are entered into that evening's draw.
